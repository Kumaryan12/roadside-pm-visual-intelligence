Please consider the manuscript “Auditing Temporal-Context Leakage in Image-Based PM2.5 Estimation: A Background-Conditioned Framework for Grouped Prediction” for publication as a Research Article in *Environmental Modelling & Software*.

This manuscript addresses a general model-evaluation problem in environmental machine learning: temporal windows constructed before random partitioning can share most of their raw context across training and test sets, producing optimistic estimates of transfer. We quantify this failure mode at the raw-frame level and connect the diagnosis to a background-conditioned modelling framework in which broad pollution state is supplied at inference and learned image and contextual models estimate a local refinement.

The study evaluates the framework on two public environmental datasets with complementary group structure. TRAQID provides 20 mobile-roadside collection dates across three seasons; a six-site Taiwan network provides 276 dates for chronological and held-out-site evaluation. The manuscript reports raw-context audits, complete-date and leave-one-site-out protocols, grouped uncertainty, operational background baselines, and calibration-assisted analyses. These elements align closely with the journal’s interests in generic environmental modelling frameworks, model evaluation, uncertainty assessment, artificial intelligence, remote sensing and air-pollution applications.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and the public data and reproducible analysis repository are identified in the paper. The author declares no competing interests.
