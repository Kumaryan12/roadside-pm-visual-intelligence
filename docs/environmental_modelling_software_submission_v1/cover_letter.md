Dear Editor,

Please consider the manuscript “A Leakage-Aware Software Framework for Background-Conditioned PM2.5 Prediction under Grouped Temporal and Spatial Shifts” for publication as a Research Article in *Environmental Modelling & Software*.

This manuscript addresses a general model-evaluation problem in environmental machine learning: temporal windows constructed before random partitioning can share most of their raw context across training and test sets, producing optimistic estimates of transfer. We quantify this failure mode at the raw-frame level and implement the response as a reusable software contract for leakage-safe sequence construction, inference-time background provenance, grouped cross-fitting, overlap auditing and group-aware uncertainty. Background-conditioned local refinement is the environmental application of that contract rather than a claim that the background-plus-residual equation is itself novel.

The study evaluates the framework retrospectively on two public environmental datasets with complementary group structure. TRAQID provides 20 mobile-roadside collection dates across three seasons; a six-site Taiwan network provides 276 dates for chronological and held-out-site evaluation. The manuscript reports raw-context audits, complete-date and leave-one-site-out protocols, matched component baselines, date- and site-cluster uncertainty, operational background requirements, outage and staleness sensitivity, and calibration-assisted analyses. These elements align closely with the journal’s interests in reusable environmental modelling software, model evaluation, uncertainty assessment, artificial intelligence, remote sensing and air-pollution applications.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and the public data and reproducible analysis repository are identified in the paper. The author declares no competing interests.
